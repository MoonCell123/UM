\section{Implementation Details}
All experiments were implemented in Pytorch and run on two NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 GPU.
We conducted with a fixed random seed of 42 for reproducibility. The teacher model was optimized for 20 epochs with a learning rate of \(1\times10^{-4}\). The subsequent fusion stage was trained for 80 epochs with the same learning rate of \(1\times10^{-4}\).
