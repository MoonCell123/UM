\begin{abstract}
The rapid development of pathological foundation models has introduced opportunities for computational pathology,
but simultaneously creates a fundamental challenge: how to understand and effectively integrate heterogeneous representations generated by multiple foundation models.
Current multi-foundation-model fusion approaches mainly rely on implicit attention or direct concatenation,
treating foundation models as independent feature sources while overlooking their sample-specific contributions and interactions.
To address this challenge, we propose [Method Name], an interpretable and plug-and-play representation fusion module that decomposes foundation model fusion into individual contribution estimation and pairwise interaction-aware refinement.
We introduce an intervention-based attribution strategy to quantify the task-specific contribution of individual foundation model.
Furthermore we devise pairwise interaction attribution (PIA) to explicitly characterize relationships among foundation models and identify complementary or redundant representation interactions.
Based on these estimates, we construct an interaction-aware joint representation module that incorporates cross-model synergistic information.
